Local-first by design

Privacy

Math Tooling Notebook does not send us your drill progress, quiz choices, function entries, or scratchpad notes. There is no account, advertising, behavioral analytics, or third-party runtime script.

Your completed drills, quiz state, and notes are stored in your browser’s localStorage under one product-specific key. This lets the notebook remember your place without a server.

What stays on your device

  • Drill completion numbers
  • Your six transfer-quiz choices and result state
  • Scratchpad text

How to remove or copy it

Use Reset local notebook at the bottom of the main page to delete all stored notebook data. Use Export .txt beside the scratchpad to download your notes. Clearing site data in your browser also removes everything.

Network and offline use

A service worker may cache the application files on your device so the notebook works offline. It contains no personal responses. Opening the site necessarily sends standard web request data (such as IP address and user agent) to the hosting provider for delivery and security logs.
