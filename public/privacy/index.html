Privacy

Your notebook data stays in your browser

Math Tooling Notebook does not send us your drill progress, quiz choices, function entries, or scratchpad notes. It has no account, advertising, behavioural analytics, or third-party runtime script.

Completed drills, quiz state, and notes use one product-specific localStorage key in your browser. This remembers your place without an account or product database.

What stays on your device

  • Completed drill numbers
  • Your six transfer-quiz choices and result state
  • Scratchpad text

How to remove or copy it

Use Reset local notebook on the main page to delete stored notebook data. Use Export .txt beside the scratchpad to download notes. Clearing site data also removes everything.

Network and offline use

A service worker may cache application files so the notebook works offline after its first visit.

Opening the site sends standard request data to the hosting provider for delivery and security logs.

Questions

For privacy questions, use the contact route on sociobot.in (external site). Last updated: 5 September 2026.
